\section{Ice-path maps of every specimen}
Figures~\ref{fig:tau_glass}--\ref{fig:tau_sand} give the map of Fig.~18 of
the paper for every specimen: ice whose geodesic path from the support is
at least $8\%$ longer than in the unloaded column at the same height is
drawn solid and coloured, the remaining ice as a faint shell.  The map is
computed on the $2\times$-binned phase maps used for the diffusion solve,
inside the grain envelope, on the largest 26-connected ice body, seeded
from a \SI{1}{\milli\meter} slab at the support face with the
\SI{4}{\milli\meter} next to the seed cut.
