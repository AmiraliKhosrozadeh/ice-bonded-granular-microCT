\section{Ice-path maps of every specimen}
Figures~\ref{fig:tau_glass}--\ref{fig:tau_sand} give the map of Fig.~18 of
the paper for every specimen.  For every ice voxel the shortest path
through the ice from the support face (scikit-image
\texttt{MCP\_Geometric}, unit cost) is compared with the same path through
the ice with the crack air of that scan closed, the air inside the grain
envelope narrower than the crack-class width of \S2.5.2 of the paper
(what a \SI{0.25}{\milli\meter} opening removes, so the wide pre-existing
pores of the $\gamma$-alumina, which are not cracks, stay open).  The
support face is an equal-potential boundary as in the diffusion solve:
every ice voxel that is the first of its axial line from the support,
within \SI{3}{\milli\meter} of it, seeds both paths.  The difference is the
detour the cracks force, zero wherever no crack lies on the way, so an
unloaded column is blank by construction and no baseline or threshold on
the unloaded scan is needed; ice detoured by more than
\SI{0.3}{\milli\meter}, after one opening and dropping bodies below
\SI{0.5}{\milli\meter\cubed}, is drawn solid and coloured by the detour,
the remaining ice as a faint shell.  The map is computed inside the grain
envelope on the largest 26-connected body of the healed ice, on the
$2\times$-binned phase maps for the beads and the full-resolution phase
maps for the sand, whose envelope is pulled in by \SI{1}{\milli\meter} to
leave out the ice-rich skin the sand packs against the tube wall.  A line
separates the unloaded scan from the loaded ones.

The paper shows G1, G3 and A4; the share of the ice detoured at every scan
is in Table~\ref{tab:taumap}.  In the glass it follows the crack volumes of
Table~\ref{tab:crack}, G1 at its first loaded step and G3 at its last far
above the rest, G2 and G5 a few per cent, G4 almost nothing.  In the
$\gamma$-alumina A4 is the largest, the two bead layers under the punch at
both steps, present already at the increment that imposed almost no
shortening, which is the punch bedding into the top layer; A2 and A3 detour
about one per cent and A1 nothing, since two thirds or more of the cracked
throats of the $\gamma$-alumina part at the grain face, a separation the
ice path runs past.  No sand specimen has crack air at any scan, so the
sand maps are blank; the loosening of S3 (Fig.~15 of the paper) thins the
ice without cutting it.
