============================
  1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
============================

Specimen: Glass beads + ice + trapped air in cylindrical holder
Software: Dragonfly 2025.1.0.2053 (Python 3.10.13, conda-forge)
Goal:     Segment CT volume into phases, create ROIs in Dragonfly,
          quantify trapped air / ice / solid fractions

Phases segmented (Glass specimen):
  Label 0 = Outside air       (gray < T_AIR_ICE, outside cylinder OR largest connected component)
  Label 1 = Trapped air       (gray < T_AIR_ICE, small air pockets inside specimen)
  Label 2 = Ice               (T_AIR_ICE <= gray < T_ICE_GLASS)
  Label 3 = Glass beads       (gray >= T_ICE_GLASS, aluminum merged into glass)

Note: Aluminum was originally a separate phase (label 4, gray >= 50000) but only
1,654 voxels were classified as aluminum — indistinguishable from glass noise.
Aluminum is now merged into glass (T_GLASS_AL = 65536, above uint16 max).
For specimens with real aluminum content, lower T_GLASS_AL to separate them.

============================
  5. SEGMENTATION PIPELINE (step by step)
============================

The pipeline has 7 steps. Steps 1-6 are pure Python (numpy, scipy, matplotlib).
Step 7 uses Dragonfly API and only runs inside Dragonfly's Jupyter console.

--- Step 1: Load cropped volume ---
  - Read TIFF files from FIRST_SLICE to LAST_SLICE
  - Crop each slice: img[Y_MIN:Y_MAX+1, X_MIN:X_MAX+1]
  - Stack into 3D numpy array: shape (Z, Y, X), dtype uint16

--- Step 2: Build TILTED cylinder mask ---
  - Cylinder axis goes from Cap1 to Cap2 (not vertical — tilted ~2.8 deg)
  - For each Z slice, interpolate the cylinder center:
      t = (z - cap1_z) / (cap2_z - cap1_z)
      cx(z) = cap1_x + t * (cap2_x - cap1_x)
      cy(z) = cap1_y + t * (cap2_y - cap1_y)
  - Circle test per slice: (x - cx(z))^2 + (y - cy(z))^2 <= radius^2
  - Only slices within Z cap range are inside the cylinder

  WHY tilted: The specimen holder is not perfectly vertical in the CT scanner.
  Using a fixed center would cut off beads at the top/bottom edges.

--- Step 3: Threshold phases inside cylinder ---
  - seg array initialized to 0 (OUTSIDE_AIR)
  - Inside cylinder:
      gray < T_AIR_ICE                  -> TRAPPED_AIR (1)
      T_AIR_ICE <= gray < T_ICE_GLASS   -> ICE (2)
      gray >= T_ICE_GLASS               -> GLASS (3)
  - Outside cylinder stays 0

--- Step 3b: Separate outside air from trapped air ---
  WHY: The cylinder mask is loose (bigger than the real specimen).
  There is a ring of air between the cylinder wall and the specimen surface.
  Without this step, all that air would be labeled "trapped air" (wrong).

  METHOD: 3D connected component labeling on all air voxels inside cylinder.
  - scipy.ndimage.label(air_mask) -> finds connected components
  - Largest component = outside air ring -> relabel to OUTSIDE_AIR (0)
  - All smaller components = real trapped air pockets -> keep as TRAPPED_AIR (1)

  This is the KEY STEP that makes the segmentation correct.

--- Step 3c: Remove salt-and-pepper noise ---
  - Ice: connected component labeling, remove clusters < MIN_ICE_VOXELS (500)
    Removed ice voxels -> reclassified as glass
  - Glass: connected component labeling, remove clusters < MIN_GLASS_VOXELS (10)
    Removed glass voxels -> reclassified as ice
    NOTE: Keep MIN_GLASS_VOXELS small! Real glass beads can be ~500 voxels.
    Setting it too high (e.g. 1000) removes real beads.
  - Trapped air: disabled by default (MIN_TRAPPED_AIR_VOXELS = 0)

--- Step 4: Statistics ---
  - Count voxels per label
  - Convert to mm3: n_voxels * (VOXEL_SIZE_UM)^3 / 1e9
  - Calculate specimen = everything except OUTSIDE_AIR
  - Ice fraction = ice / specimen * 100
  - Pore fraction = (ice + trapped_air) / specimen * 100

--- Step 5: Save TIFF stack ---
  - Display values: seg * 50 -> 0/50/100/150 (uint8)
  - Saved to OUT_DIR as seg_0000.tif, seg_0001.tif, ...

--- Step 6: Save overview PNG ---
  - 3 orthogonal slices (XY, XZ, YZ) at volume center
  - Color-coded: black=outside air, red=trapped air, blue=ice, tan=glass
  - Saved as segmentation_overview.png

--- Step 7: Create ROIs in Dragonfly ---
  (Only runs inside Dragonfly Jupyter console)
  See Section 7 below for full details.
  Creates 3 ROIs: ROI_TrappedAir, ROI_Ice, ROI_GlassBeads

============================
  7. DRAGONFLY API — COMPLETE REFERENCE
============================

All of this was discovered through trial and error in Dragonfly 2025.1.0.2053.
These patterns are CONFIRMED WORKING. Do not guess alternatives.

--- 7a. Imports ---

  from ORSModel import createChannelFromNumpyArray, ROI, orsColor, Channel

  # WorkingContext (for reference -- NOT usable from scripts):
  # from OrsLibraries.workingcontext import WorkingContext
  # All WorkingContext methods need a plugin instance as first arg.
  # Cannot be used from standalone scripts or Jupyter console.

--- 7b. Find existing channels in workspace ---

  cls_name = Channel.getClassNameStatic()           # returns 'CxvChannel'
  all_channels = Channel.getAllObjectsOfClass(cls_name)  # NEEDS cls_name string!
  for ch in all_channels:
      title = ch.getTitle()     # display title (e.g. 'Glass-1001800-T5-...')
      # NOT ch.getName() which returns UUID string like 'orsObj(B2CC4AE1...)'

--- 7c. Create temporary channel from numpy ---

  seg_ch = createChannelFromNumpyArray(arr.astype(np.uint8))
  seg_ch.setTitle('_seg_temp_delete_me')
  seg_ch.publish()     # REQUIRED -- getAsROIWithinRange only works on published channels

--- 7d. Extract ROI from channel ---

  roi_tmp = ROI()
  roi_tmp.copyShapeFromStructuredGrid(seg_ch)    # MUST match source channel dimensions
  seg_ch.getAsROIWithinRange(vmin, vmax, None, roi_tmp)
  # vmin, vmax = raw integer thresholds (e.g. 49, 51 for value 50)
  # Normalized thresholds (0-1) did NOT work in our testing

--- 7e. Remap ROI to CT channel coordinates (CRITICAL for 2D visibility) ---

  roi_ct = roi_tmp.makeROIForChannel(ct_channel, 0, 0, 0)
  # Arguments: (target_channel, x_offset, y_offset, z_offset)
  # Offsets (0,0,0) because seg and CT cover the same cropped volume
  # Returns a NEW ROI object in the target channel's coordinate system

  roi_ct.setTitle('ROI_Ice')
  roi_ct.setInitialColor(orsColor(0.4, 0.7, 1.0, 1.0))   # RGBA
  roi_ct.publish()

  WHY this step is needed:
  - createChannelFromNumpyArray creates a channel with DEFAULT coordinates
  - The CT channel has REAL physical coordinates (origin, voxel spacing)
  - Without remapping, ROI exists at wrong location -> invisible in 2D views
  - makeROIForChannel transforms ROI to target channel's coordinate system

--- 7f. Alternative methods (for reference) ---

  adaptToChannel(pChannel, x, y, z, pTSourceOffset, pTRange)
    # pTSourceOffset=0, pTRange=1 for single-frame volumes
    # Modifies ROI in-place (does not return a new ROI)

  getAsROIMovedInChannel(pInputData, xOffset, yOffset, zOffset, pTargetROI)
    # Moves ROI by voxel offset within a channel

--- 7g. Delete objects ---

  obj.deleteObject()    # works for Channel and ROI
  # NOT obj.remove() -- that also works but deleteObject() is documented

--- 7h. Methods that DO NOT EXIST ---

  ROI.getArray()              -- no NumPy access on ROI objects
  ROI.show()                  -- does not exist in Dragonfly 2025.1
  WorkingContext.getCurrentWorkingContext()  -- does not exist
  Channel.getName()           -- returns UUID, not display title (use getTitle())

--- 7i. Physical coordinate methods on channels ---

  ct_channel.getOrigin()      -> orsVect(x, y, z) in meters
  ct_channel.getXSpacing()    -> voxel size in X (meters)
  ct_channel.getYSpacing()    -> voxel size in Y (meters)
  ct_channel.getZSpacing()    -> voxel size in Z (meters)
  ct_channel.setOrigin(x, y, z)
  ct_channel.setXSpacing(val)
  ct_channel.setYSpacing(val)
  ct_channel.setZSpacing(val)

============================
  9. ADAPTING FOR OTHER SPECIMENS (Alumina, Sand, etc.)
============================

To reuse this pipeline for a different specimen, change these parameters:

--- 9a. What MUST change ---

  TIFF_DIR        Path to raw TIFF folder
  FILE_PREFIX     TIFF filename prefix (before the 4-digit number)
  FIRST_SLICE     First TIFF file number
  LAST_SLICE      Last TIFF file number

  X_MIN, X_MAX    Crop bounds in X (from Dragonfly crop operation)
  Y_MIN, Y_MAX    Crop bounds in Y

  Cylinder parameters (world coordinates in um from Dragonfly):
    CYL_CAP1_UM     (X, Y, Z) of cap1 in um
    CYL_CAP2_UM     (X, Y, Z) of cap2 in um
    CYL_RADIUS_UM   radius in um
    CT_ORIGIN_UM    CT channel origin in um (from ct_channel.getOrigin() * 1e6)
    The script automatically converts to voxel coordinates and handles tilt.

  T_AIR_ICE       Threshold between air and first solid phase
  T_ICE_GLASS     Threshold between phases
  T_GLASS_AL      Threshold for densest phase (set to 65536 to disable)

  VOXEL_SIZE_UM   Voxel size in micrometers (isotropic)

  OUT_DIR         Output directory for segmentation TIFFs
  overview_path   Path for overview PNG

  CT channel name in Step 7: change 'Glass-1001800' to match new dataset name

--- 9b. What MAY change ---

  LABEL_NAMES     Phase names (e.g. 'Sand' instead of 'Glass beads')
  phase_defs      ROI names and colors in Step 7
  Number of phases (may need to add/remove thresholds and labels)
  MIN_ICE_VOXELS, MIN_GLASS_VOXELS  Noise removal thresholds

--- 9c. What stays the same ---

  The pipeline structure (Steps 1-7)
  The tilted cylinder masking approach
  The connected component method for separating outside air from trapped air
  The noise removal method (Step 3c)
  The Dragonfly API patterns (Section 7)
  The TIFF scaling (x50)

--- 9d. How to determine new thresholds ---

  1. Run histogram_analysis.py on the new dataset (or write a new one)
  2. Look for peaks in the histogram -> each peak = one phase
  3. Set thresholds at the valleys between peaks
  4. For quick check: open a few TIFF slices in ImageJ/Fiji, use the histogram tool

--- 9e. How to get cylinder parameters from Dragonfly ---

  1. Load raw TIFFs in Dragonfly
  2. Use Dragonfly's crop tool to select the region of interest
  3. Read crop bounds from Dragonfly's macro log (report.txt / Properties panel)
  4. Create a cylinder ROI in Dragonfly around the specimen
  5. Read cylinder cap centers and radius from Dragonfly Properties panel:
       Cap1 center: (X, Y, Z) in um
       Cap2 center: (X, Y, Z) in um
       Radius: um
  6. Get CT channel origin: run in Dragonfly console:
       print(ct_channel.getOrigin())   # returns meters, multiply by 1e6 for um
  7. Enter these values directly in the script as CYL_CAP1_UM, CYL_CAP2_UM, etc.
     The script automatically converts to cropped voxel coordinates.

--- 9f. Noise removal guidelines ---

  MIN_ICE_VOXELS:   500 works for glass specimen. Increase for noisier data.
  MIN_GLASS_VOXELS: Keep SMALL (10-50). Real beads can be ~500 voxels.
                    Setting too high removes real beads!
  MIN_TRAPPED_AIR_VOXELS: Usually 0 (disabled). Enable to clean air noise.


============================
  10. TROUBLESHOOTING
============================

--- UnicodeDecodeError on exec() ---
  Use encoding='utf-8':
  exec(open(path, encoding='utf-8').read(), globals())

--- ROIs appear but are empty in 2D ---
  The ROI coordinate system doesn't match the CT channel.
  MUST use makeROIForChannel(ct_channel, 0, 0, 0) after extraction.

--- ROIs fill the whole screen ---
  Same coordinate mismatch problem. See above.

--- Beads cut off at top/bottom of specimen ---
  The cylinder is probably tilted. Use CYL_CAP1_UM and CYL_CAP2_UM
  with different X values to follow the tilt. The script interpolates
  the center per Z slice automatically.

--- Glass beads missing from ROI ---
  MIN_GLASS_VOXELS too high. Real beads can be small (~500 voxels).
  Lower MIN_GLASS_VOXELS to 10 or 0.

--- Aluminum indistinguishable from glass ---
  Set T_GLASS_AL = 65536 to merge aluminum into glass.
  Only separate them if the histogram shows a clear aluminum peak.

--- "import failed" in Step 7 ---
  Step 7 only works inside Dragonfly's Jupyter console, not standalone Python.

--- CT channel not found ---
  Make sure the CT dataset is loaded in Dragonfly before running the script.
  Check that the title search string matches ('Glass-1001800' for glass dataset).

--- getAsROIWithinRange produces empty ROIs ---
  Check that seg_ch is published (seg_ch.publish() must be called first).
  Check threshold values are raw integers, not normalized (0-1).

--- Segmentation looks wrong (too much trapped air) ---
  Step 3b connected component method may need adjustment.
  The largest air component inside the cylinder = outside air ring.
  If specimen has a very large internal void, it might be misclassified.
  Solution: check component sizes, possibly use top-N largest as outside air.

--- _seg_temp_delete_me channel stays in workspace ---
  deleteObject() or remove() failed. Delete it manually in Dragonfly.
