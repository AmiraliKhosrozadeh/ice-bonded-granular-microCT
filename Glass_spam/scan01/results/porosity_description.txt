SCAN 1 POROSITY ANALYSIS — DEFINITIONS AND FILES
============================================================

Glass bead / ice / trapped air CT specimen (pre-compression).
Aluminum is shown in seg for presentation but EXCLUDED from
all fractions — the analysis is about the ice+glass+air cylinder.

INTERIOR DEFINITION
----------------------------------------
Interior = ice + glass + trapped air (aluminum excluded).
Given by specimen_mask from scan01_segmentation.py.

POROSITY DEFINITIONS (denominator = interior voxels)
----------------------------------------

1. TOTAL POROSITY (ice + trapped air) / interior
   = all void space between glass beads, regardless of fill.
   Overall value: 44.46%
   Files: porosity_total_radial.png, porosity_total_height.png

2. AIR-FILLED POROSITY (trapped air) / interior
   = fraction of pore space that remained unfrozen.
   Overall value: 0.25%
   Files: porosity_air_radial.png, porosity_air_height.png

3. ICE FRACTION (ice) / interior
   = fraction of interior occupied by ice.
   Overall value: 44.21%
   Files: porosity_ice_radial.png, porosity_ice_height.png

4. PACKING FRACTION (glass) / interior
   = fraction of interior occupied by glass beads.
   packing + total porosity = 100%
   Overall value: 55.54%
   Files: porosity_packing_radial.png, porosity_packing_height.png

RADIAL PROFILES
----------------------------------------
Porosity vs normalized radius (0 = cylinder axis, 1 = reference wall).
Shows the wall effect for packed beds.
Reference: Sadeq et al. (2024) Fuel Processing Technology 265, 108149.

HEIGHT PROFILES
----------------------------------------
Porosity vs normalized height (0 = top, 1 = bottom).
